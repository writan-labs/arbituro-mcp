## Work tracked in Arbituro

This repository's work is tracked in Arbituro, project `ARB`. Replace `ARB` with your project's key.

- Before starting a job, read it with the Arbituro MCP server's `get_job`. Its done-when is the
  contract: the job is finished when every clause is true.
- Work on the branch the job names. Any branch whose name carries the job's key counts, for example
  `arb-12-copy-a-jobs-link`.
- Put the job's key in every commit message and in the pull request's title, for example
  `ARB-12: copy a job's link`.
- Do not tick, change or finish anything in Arbituro. Claim a clause with what you checked, and a
  person decides.
- When you stop, leave a note on the job: what you did, what is left, and where it is.
